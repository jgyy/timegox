# Ascension of the Azure Cloud: notes for AI contributors

## Art rule
**All art is generated from scripts.** Every model, texture, animation and effect must be reproducible by running code in this repository. Do not add hand-made, downloaded or AI-image-generated asset files. Screenshots in `docs/screenshots` are outputs, not inputs.

- **Godot code** (`scripts/`, GDScript and shaders): anything built from game data or changing at runtime, such as terrain, weather, spell effects, shaders and UI.
- **Blender Python** (`art/`, `bpy` for the latest Blender release): hero assets such as characters, monsters, rigs, animations and baked textures. Each script exports a `.glb` into `assets/` via `blender --background --python <script>`. Exported files are rebuilt from their scripts and never edited by hand.
- `art/lib/kit.py` is the shared toolkit (Godot-space coordinates, tint UV, toon/plain/glow material slots, baked AO, rigs, clips); `art/lib/person.py` and `art/lib/creature.py` build characters and monsters. `python art/build_all.py` (with `pip install bpy`) rebuilds everything; run `godot --headless --path . --import` afterwards.
- **Exports are packed.** `kit.export` runs `art/lib/pack.py` on every `.glb` it writes (byte weights, 16-bit tint UVs and bone rotations, no tracks for bones that rest, shared time tracks, compact JSON: about a third smaller, and Godot imports the same meshes and clips). Never commit a raw export; `python art/pack_all.py` packs anything exported with `ART_PACK=0`. Do not quantise normals or positions: Godot's importer refuses `KHR_mesh_quantization`.
- After any art change, render and look at screenshots: `godot --path . --fixed-fps 30 -- --tour=<dir> --areas=<id> --no-extras --gallery` (use `xvfb-run` on a server).
- **Screenshots are WebP, never PNG.** Every capture the tour, the gallery or the bot writes is saved with `Image.save_webp(path, true, 0.9)` (lossy, quality 0.9: visually the same as the PNG, 4 to 10 times smaller). Name new captures `.webp` and link them from the README with `.webp`. Do not commit PNG screenshots; if one turns up, convert it with `magick in.png -quality 90 -define webp:method=6 out.webp` and delete the PNG.

## Trials (many roads)
- The bosses that carry the main story forward are guarded by **trials** in `scripts/trials.gd`: each has three or four ways through (might, craft, wealth, respect, Dao, friendship, relic), and only a met way can be taken. A new chapter's lords should get trials too, with recipes whose essences can be hunted on the way and quest items found before them (`tests/run_tests.gd` checks this).
- Give every new trial a planned way in the bot's `TRIAL_PLAN` (`scripts/bot.gd`) that it can actually work towards, and keep respect thresholds above the renown the bosses and trials alone give.

## Crossroads, works and the hours of the wild
- **Choices are crossroads** (`scripts/echoes.gd`): a question asked once after a step, whose answers tip one of three scales (mercy and rigour, new works and old ways, the wide world and the home fire), give something, **echo** in later conversations (`echo`: a dialogue key later in the story -> lines for each answer) and are told at the end (`end`). A new chapter should have at least one crossroads of its own, give earlier ones an echo in its conversations, and give the bot an answer in `CROSS_PLAN` (`scripts/bot.gd`); `tests/run_tests.gd` checks every echo lands in a real, later conversation and fits the dialogue box. Conversations are fetched through `Echoes.told`, never straight from `Data.DIALOGUE`.
- **Technology is works** (`scripts/works.gd`, `scripts/machines.gd`): eighteen things to build in three schools (the Hand, the Wheel, the Art), paid for in know-how (learned by looking: bestiary, lords, things tried, obstacles, deeds, caves) and spirit stones. Each changes one number through `Works.mod` / `Works.add` and stands in the land as a machine built from code. A journey's know-how must never buy all eighteen (the test checks it), so that which to build stays a choice. Trials can be passed "By the works" (`["works", school or "" or "best", n]`).
- **Monsters keep hours** (`scripts/habits.gd`): every new enemy gets a habit from its shape and element (or an entry in `Habits.OWN`), and what a pack is doing at the hour it is met (asleep, hunting, resting, gathered) is drawn in the land and changes the battle. Bosses keep no hours.

## Caves, rooms and things to do
- **The ground is hollow** (`scripts/voxel.gd`, `scripts/caves.gd`): every region but the sect's own courtyard has a cave made of voxels (half a metre a side, four to a tile), laid out from the region's seed and entered by a cave mouth that `Caves.place` stands on open ground. A cave is a floor of its realm (`f:<area>:cave:-1`, a lower gallery `-2`), like a cellar: its own tiles, people and things. A new region gets one by itself (give its theme a look in `Caves.LOOKS` / `LOOK_OF`); a cave of the story gets an entry in `Caves.OWN` (name, size, where its mouth should stand, what lives in it, a lower gallery).
- **What the story puts in a cave is written with its region** in `Data.AREAS` like everybody else, with `"late": true, "cave": -1` (or `-2`) and `"stand": "deep" | "mid" | "near" | "any"`; `Caves.world` stands it on free ground. A step done in a cave carries `"cave": -1`; a hunt there cannot ask for the first strike (the sword's wake is for the open land). New step kinds: `"delve"` (go to the very back of n caves) and `"try"` (do n things of a cave, or the `things` named). The bot goes down by itself (`_goto_layer`, `_fetch`, `_delve_step`, `_try_step`). Trials can be passed "By the dark" (`["delved", n]`).
- **Only the lay of a cave (tiles, levels, roofs) is made when the world is built**; the voxels are made and meshed when somebody walks in (`Caves.volume`, `Voxel.mesh`, in pieces of six tiles so that the renderer's eight lights per mesh are enough). Keep it that way: the bot and the tests never need the rock.
- **Every building has a cellar and, unless its roof takes none, an upper storey** (`scripts/rooms.gd`): floors written by hand in `Data.AREAS` stay as they are, the rest are laid out from the building's rectangle. A new building style needs an entry in `Rooms.UPPER` and `Rooms.CELLAR` (or in `NO_UPPER`); new furniture needs a kind in `Props.KINDS`, a line in `Props.FURNITURE` and a model in `Rooms.furnish` or `Architecture._furnish`.
- **A new thing to do** needs its clip in `art/lib/person.py` (`INTERACT`, so the party has it; add it to `Assets.LEND` if townsfolk are to borrow it), what is held in `Props.HELD` with a model, and three lines of its own. Gestures everybody has go in `person.CLIPS` and `Data.EMOTES`: that rebuilds every character.

## Mountains, climbing, the cloud-kite and the people of the road
- **The mountains are a term in the height field** (`scripts/peaks.gd`): `Peaks.place` gives every region peaks from its seed, on ground nothing stands on and wholly inside the region's own land (so no peak joins two regions and the seals keep what they kept), and `Peaks.rise` (a few sines, no mesh) is added in `Terrain3D.world_heights`. Everything that asks how high the ground is asks `Peaks.rise` / `Peaks.at`: the bot and the tests climb and glide without drawing anything. A region's peaks are numbered from its highest down (`peak:<area>:<k>`); a peak of the story gets its name in `Peaks.OWN`.
- **A step that rises more than `Peaks.STEEP` is climbed** (`World.climb`, the clip `scale`), and **V opens the cloud-kite** (`World.toggle_glide`, `scripts/glider.gd`, the clips `glide` and `alight`): a flight loses `GLIDE_SINK` metres a tile, never crosses into a region he has not walked into (`Game.seen`), a closed seal or the open sea, passes over a wood, a roof or anybody standing only with air to spare (`GLIDE_OVER`), and ends only where somebody can stand and walk away (`_landable`). Keep it so: gliding must never be a way round the story.
- **What the story puts on a peak is written with its region** in `Data.AREAS` like everybody else, with `"late": true, "peak": k` and `"stand": "top" | "flank" | "foot"`; `Peaks.world` stands it there (the very top tile is left for whoever climbs). New step kinds: `"summit"` (`"peak": area` and `"k"`, or `"n"` summits, any of them) and `"glide"` (one flight of `n` tiles). Trials can be passed "By the heights" (`["summits", n]`). The bot climbs and flies by itself (`_summit_step`, `_glide_step`).
- **The land between the towns is lived in** (`scripts/folk.gd`, `CAMP_ROLES`): a camp at the foot of the highest peak of every region that has room for one (`Folk.place_camps`), whose seven people (woodcutter, herbalist, angler, pedlar, hunter, pilgrim, hermit) are made, moved and talked to by everything that makes the people of the towns: a camp is a town with no houses (`Folk.town`, `Folk.homes_in`). A new kind of person of the road needs a day in `Folk.ROLES` that never goes `"in"`, places in `Folk.PLACES`, bodies, a title and at least four lines. An errand among them is a `"folk"` step whose `role` is one of theirs.
- A region may have **a second war-band for a later chapter** (`"warband2"` in `Data.AREAS`).

## Faces, arms and the ends of the story
- **Every human head has six bones of expression** that no clip moves (`art/lib/face.py`: `brow.L`, `brow.R`, `lip.L`, `lip.R`, `lids` over the eye bone, `jaw` over the mouth bone), with the brows and lips weighted to them wholly and the skin under them partly. **A face is a pose of those bones written in `scripts/faces.gd`** (`EXPRESSIONS`), worn over whatever clip plays: `Faces.wear(model, id, seconds)`, `Faces.keep(model, id)`. A new gesture or battle action gets a face in `Faces.OF_ACT`, a new thing to do in `Faces.OF_CLIP`; a line of dialogue may name one as a fourth element (`["Speaker", "text", "nod", "sorrow"]`), otherwise its words are read for one. Do not key the six bones in a clip, and do not write morph targets (`pack.py` refuses them).
- **The hero's weapon is one of the Eight Arms** (`scripts/arms.gd`, models in `art/lib/arms.py` exported by `art/weapons/<id>.py`, standing on the grip with their length along +Y). The hero's models carry a `sword` bone and no blade of their own (`"arms": True`); `Arms.attach` hangs the weapon there, so a clip that stows the sword stows any of them. **A new weapon needs** a model, an entry in `Arms.WEAPONS` (its Dao, its `mods` by rank, where it is won), a clip for its blow and one for its technique in `person.ARMS` (`arm_<id>`, `art_<id>`: held poses eased by `_keyed`), and a technique in `Data.SKILLS` with `"arm"`, `k` and `ck` (measured against what the hero already knows: `Game.skill_of`). Each weapon keeps its own mastery (`Game.arm_xp`); the tests check that no two have the same blow, technique or Dao.
- A hunt may ask for a weapon (`"arm": "<id>"`: only what falls while it is in the hand counts), a step may ask for Daos of arms (`"kind": "arms", "n": 3`), and trials can be passed "By the hand" (`["arms", n]`). The bot changes weapons by itself (`_want_arm`): give it nothing to do for a new one but own it.
- **The story has twelve ends** (`Cultures.ENDINGS`), and not all of them are kind: `kind` is `"bright"`, `"bitter"` or `"dark"`, and a bitter or dark end says so at the start of its `desc`, has `"hearth": false`, three lines of its own, a last word in `Ending.LAST_WORDS` and, if it is not an ascension, a heading for the results in `Ending.HEADLINES`. An end is open only to the journey that earned it (`needs`: hearths, peoples, Yue Ling, Daos of arms, works, the way a scale of the crossroads leans, or having sat at nobody's table). A new chapter's last crossroads should tip the scales the ends ask about.

## Hearths (the six cultures) and who the hero is
- The peoples of the land are the **Six Hearths** in `scripts/cultures.gd` (towns, keepers, greetings, tables, fires, customs, standing, the six beginnings and the five endings). A new town of the land belongs to a hearth (`towns`, `areas`); a new chapter's errands for a hearth carry `"hearth": "<id>"` on their steps, done in that hearth's own way, and an errand among its townsfolk names its `"town"`.
- The hero can be a man or a woman. **Write story text about "Lin Feng" and "he" as always**; `scripts/voice.gd` tells it of her on the way to the screen (narration by rule, speech only by what the hero is called). Where the rule gets a new line wrong, add the whole line to `Voice.FIX`; `tests/run_tests.gd` reads every conversation through it. Words about nobody but the hero (an outfit, a ride) go through `Voice.her`.
- New people need a Blender script in `art/characters/`; women are marked `"fem": True` there and, if they are named without a woman's title, listed in `Voice.WOMEN`.

## Performance
- `godot --path . -- --bench=build/bench.json` (with `xvfb-run` on a server) times the opening of the world and counts what a frame draws; `--census` lists the triangles in the scene by kind (`--census-view`: only what the camera sees). Compare draw calls and primitives between two builds on the same machine; the frame rate of a software renderer says little.
- Keep the importer's LODs when a mesh is rebuilt (`Assets.lods_of`), give anything heavy that is scattered out to 100 m a far mesh (`Terrain3D.far_mesh`), and do not hand the renderer people or things further off than they can be made out (`World.ENT_SHOW`). `Data.step_index` is a lookup: `Game.reached` is asked thousands of times a frame.

## Checks before pushing
- `godot --headless --path . --script tests/run_tests.gd`
- `godot --headless --path . --script tests/manual_input_test.gd`
- `godot --headless --fixed-fps 30 --path . -- --bot --seed=1234 --min-time=57600` (CI requires the full game to complete in at least 16 hours of game time)
